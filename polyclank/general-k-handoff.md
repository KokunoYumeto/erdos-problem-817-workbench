## Objective and actual outcome

The objective is the general progression-length problem for distinct positive
integer generators, not another verification of the k=4 upper construction.
The contribution proves existence of the exponential rate for every fixed
k>=3, identifies that rate exactly as an infimum of finite cyclic certificate
costs, and supplies a full finite carry decision procedure for all word lengths.
The carry theorem retains noncanonical actual digit values and negative carries.
It is accompanied by explicit witness maps in both directions.

The shared six-generator block {1,4,5,17,21,22} gives rates 97^(1/6) for k=5
and 93^(1/6) for k=6. Both improve the corresponding upper bounds of Korsky's
inspected arXiv:2606.24139v1. The base 93 certificate checks composite-modulus
steps, including steps of nontrivial additive order. These finite certificates
are sufficient for the all-length constructions proved in the note.

Exact numerical values of the rates for all k and the sharp large-k lower law
remain undetermined by this contribution. The missing uniform inequality is
stated precisely in Section 10 of the note. No theorem here assumes it.

## Research rationale and exact obstruction handling

The k=4 coefficient-layer splitting has an explicit failure at k=5:
{1,2} is 5-admissible but its short relation produces the allowed four-term
progression (0,1,2,3). The replacement keeps all simultaneous progression
conditions through product decompositions and finite carry states.

The composition uses R=2*sum(A)+1 and the bijection (x,y)->x+R*y on actual
subset-sum images. Its inverse is the displayed remainder/quotient pair.
The cost 2*sum(A)+1 is exactly multiplicative. This supplies the all-k limit
and the cofinal finite modular embeddings without assuming a rate formula.

The strict distinction between modular freeness and carry freeness is made
constructive: {2,7} in base 11 has modular three-term progressions but its
three-state carry closure excludes every integer progression at every length.
A separate noncanonical-digit example requires a negative carry, so dropping
those carries would lose a real failure witness.

The six-generator vector model retains endpoint correlations discarded by a
rectangular-box bound. The latter has a proved column-mass obstruction; the
actual correlated image and its modular certificates bypass that restricted
representation requirement. This identifies a productive distinction rather
than declaring the general target impossible.
