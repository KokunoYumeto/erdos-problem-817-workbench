\section{2. Relative cochains, original primitives and arithmetic feasible sets}\label{n13-relative-cochains-original-primitives-and-arithmetic-feasible-sets}

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 0\to R^{2r}\xrightarrow{\delta^0}R^{3r}
\xrightarrow D H^1(\Theta_r,\{u,v\};R)\cong R^r\to0,$
\end{adjustbox}

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \delta^0(\alpha_j,\beta_j)

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}

\section{4. Infinite graphical filtration and finite receiving support}\label{n13-infinite-graphical-filtration-and-finite-receiving-support}

For any finite loopless multigraph G, write Sigma(G) for its actual orientation scores. The integral span of directions of (q+1)-term source progressions is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle K_q(G)=\{z\in\mathbb Z^V:\sum_{v\in C}z_v=0
\text{ for every q-edge-connected class }C\}.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The proof uses the classical Gomory--Hu cut-tree theorem. Low cut capacities force the indicated zero charges, and edge-disjoint paths supply actual progressions for every class-root generator. Integral-span combinations are not declared feasible directions without their original source witnesses.

For an actual mark observation L\_T, its image is the ideal

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle I_{q,T}=a_q\mathbb Z,\qquad
 a_q=\gcd\{|t_u-t_v|:u,v\text{ belong to the same q-class}\}.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The original exact sequence is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 0\to K_q\cap\ker L_T\to K_q\xrightarrow{L_T}a_q\mathbb Z\to0.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

A nonconstant visible source progression exists exactly when a\_q\textgreater0. When all its source directions have zero observed action, the source K\_q remains; it has not become an absent support.

The entire q-family is reconstructed on the semilattice of positive integers with reverse order, together with bottom infinity and join min.
