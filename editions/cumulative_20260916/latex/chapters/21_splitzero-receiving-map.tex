\section{1. The original word relation and its further evaluation relation}\label{n21-the-original-word-relation-and-its-further-evaluation-relation}

At a specified q\textgreater=2, base b\textgreater sum(B), and horizon m, retain every original q-digit generator word

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \Omega_m=\{0,\ldots,q-1\}^{m|B|}.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Partitioning its coordinates by their original levels gives its local value quotient. Write D=H\_q(B) and let omega(d) count the original block representations. There are surjections

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \mathbb Q[\Omega_m]\xrightarrow{q_m}\mathbb Q[D^m]
\xrightarrow{J_m}\mathbb Q[H_q(B_m)],
\qquad J_me_{(d_j)}=e_{\sum_jb^jd_j}.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Let B\_m\^{}loc=ker(q\_m) and B\_m\^{}tot=ker(J\_mq\_m). The comparison is the exact sequence

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 0\to B_m^{loc}\to B_m^{tot}\xrightarrow{q_m}\ker J_m\to0.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Surjectivity of the last map follows by choosing an original block representative of every local value. The receiving kernel is therefore \(B_m^{\mathrm{tot}}/B_m^{\mathrm{loc}}\), with its actual inclusion and quotient maps, not a reassignment of the original relation module.

The predecessor\textquotesingle s canonical binary components for the base97 six-generator family are its original six-generator levels. All of them join in the ternary factorization, witnessed by

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 97=1+2\cdot5+2\cdot21+2\cdot22.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The analogous actual bounded relations for bases93 and1651 are retained in the full note. The additional ternary kernel dimensions at two of the bases are exactly

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 139^m-\frac{68\,97^m-21\,3^m}{47},$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle 2103^m-\frac{1007\,1651^m-226\,89^m}{781}.$
\end{adjustbox}

This calculates the exponential growth of the extra receiving relation space. Every local image value remains represented before that quotient.

For support bookkeeping, use the finite admitted subsets of the actual word set, with inclusion transports and union as join, and the original value images of those subsets. \section{2. Full original metrics and an exact finite certificate for every horizon}\label{n21-full-original-metrics-and-an-exact-finite-certificate-for-every-horizon}

The original orthonormal word metric gives local Gram

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle G_{loc}=\operatorname{diag}_{d\in D^m}\left(1/\prod_j\omega(d_j)\right).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Let mu\_m(x)=sum\_(ev(d)=x) product\_j omega(d\_j). The minimum-section formula is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle s_me_x=\sum_{ev(d)=x}\frac{\prod_j\omega(d_j)}{\mu_m(x)}e_d,
\qquad G_{tot}=\operatorname{diag}_x(1/\mu_m(x)).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The first identity has J\_ms\_m=1. A vector in ker J\_m has zero coefficient sum on each final fibre, so it is orthogonal to each displayed section vector in G\_loc. Thus every original lift z of v satisfies

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \|z\|_{G_{loc}}^2=\|z-s_mv\|_{G_{loc}}^2+\|v\|_{G_{tot}}^2.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The exact source carries d+c=z+bc\textquotesingle{} have states c=0,1 in the ternary cases. For original multiplicities their residue matrix is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle W_z(c,c')=\omega(z+bc'-c).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The full residue-class mass certificate bounds every row sum by 15 for the six-generator blocks and by219 for the ten-generator block. The original digits70 and1102 achieve these weights without a carry. The matrix bound and the repeated-digit lower witness prove

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \max_x\mu_m(x)=15^m\quad\text{or}\quad219^m.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

The original quotient-to-unit-value return norms are therefore exactly 15\^{}(m/2) and219\^{}(m/2). Starting instead from orthonormal \emph{distinct local values} gives maximum carry-fibre size 2\^{}(m-1) and return norm 2\^{}((m-1)/2). The two sources are connected by their original weighted sections; their norms are not silently equated or multiplied as if the worst fibres coincided.

\section{3. A finite observation that removes only its actual invisible directions}\label{n21-a-finite-observation-that-removes-only-its-actual-invisible-directions}

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle T_z(R)=\{c':d+c=z+bc',\ d\in D,\ c\in R\}.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

This is the map from original representations to distinct values; it counts neither a residue nor a path twice.

\[

$\displaystyle Jv=(f^Tv,f^TTv,\ldots,f^TT^{s-1}v)$

\section{4. Infinite real completion and the original terminal boundary}\label{n21-infinite-real-completion-and-the-original-terminal-boundary}

For a finite digit set D subset {[}0,b-2{]}, the actual real limit is K=\{sum\_(j\textgreater=1)d\_j b\^{}(-j)\}. The finite high-prefix carry is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle c_{j+1}=bc_j+\Delta d_j,
\qquad |c_j|\le 2\max(D)/(b-1).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

A finite graph therefore decides existence of a real k-AP: a reachable true-flag state must have an infinite continuation, equivalently reach a cycle. If such a progression exists, the same graph supplies rational coordinates with an eventually periodic expansion. The proof retains the real-tail equation and differs from the finite integer test, which requires the terminal second difference to be exactly zero.

For the six-generator base97 example, take

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle a=(4,5,5,5,6),\qquad e=(64,0,32,64,0).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Every entry has an original binary subset representative. The real point tuple is

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle x=\frac1{97}\left(a+\frac e{96}\right)
=\frac1{291}(14,15,16,17,18).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Its finite integer representatives

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle Y_m=97^{m-1}a+\frac{97^{m-1}-1}{96}e$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

obey

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \Delta Y_m=(-1,0,1)\ne0\quad\text{for every }m,
\qquad97^{-m}Y_m\to x.$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Thus the boundary remains supported at every finite level; scaling makes its observed norm tend to zero. The full note supplies equally explicit real4-,6-, and6-term witnesses for the base19,93,1651 constructions, respectively.

The additional observation can be kept literally. On sequences for which both limits exist, let

\[
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=.92\linewidth}
$\displaystyle \mathcal O((y_m))=(\lim b^{-m}y_m,\lim\Delta y_m).$
\end{adjustbox}
\]

Its image is ker Delta direct-sum R\^{}(k-2). For c in R\^{}(k-2), define R(c)\_0=R(c)\_1=0 and \(R(c)_i=\sum_{j\le i-2}(i-1-j)c_j\). Forgetting the second component has precisely kernel 0 direct-sum R\^{}(k-2).
