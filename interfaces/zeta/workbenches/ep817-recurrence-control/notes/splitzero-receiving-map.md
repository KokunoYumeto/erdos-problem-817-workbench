## 4. Infinite cochain recovery with an evaluated uniform remainder

For a Hilbert coefficient space E with its given form G, let S be the unilateral right shift on ell^2(N_0;E). The full boundary operator is

\[
D_r=-2I+S-(S^*)^{r-1}.
\]

Direct expansion in the original inner product gives, with m=r-1,

\[
-\operatorname{Re}\langle D_rx,x\rangle
=\tfrac12\bigl(\|(I-S)x\|^2+\|(I+S^m)x\|^2\bigr)
\ge 2\|x\|^2/m^2.
\]

The inequality follows from I-S^m=(I+...+S^(m-1))(I-S) and the parallelogram identity for x and S^m x. It also holds for D_r^*. Therefore D_r has closed dense range and is invertible, with

\[
\|D_r^{-1}\|\le m^2/2.
\]

For a nonzero coefficient space, the r=2 norm is exactly 1/2. For r>=3, truncated plane waves give the lower bound (r-2)^2/pi^2. These two explicit bounds identify quadratic delay growth. The zero coefficient fibre retains its unique bijection with norm zero; a lower-norm assertion is made only for a nonzero fibre.

The original full inverse is also

\[
D_r^{-1}=-\int_0^\infty e^{tD_r}\,dt,
\quad
\left\|D_r^{-1}+\int_0^T e^{tD_r}\,dt\right\|
\le\frac{m^2}{2}e^{-2T/m^2}.

Differentiate ||exp(tD_r)x||^2 to get its stated exponential bound, integrate the derivative to verify both inverse laws, and integrate the bound for the remainder. All constants retain their delay dependence and are uniform in coefficient dimension and the infinite horizon.
