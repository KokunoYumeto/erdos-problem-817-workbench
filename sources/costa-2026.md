# Simone Costa, 2026: separate `k = 3` context

- Exact identity: Simone Costa, “A negative answer to the Erdős–Sárkőzy
  question,” arXiv:2609.06303v1 [math.CO], submitted 5 September 2026.
- Primary locator: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06303>
- Theorem used for scope only:
  `liminf_(n→∞) g_3(n)/3^n = 0`; more precisely, for every positive
  `epsilon`, some integer `d ≥ 2` satisfies
  `g_3(d*l) ≤ epsilon*3^(d*l)` for every sufficiently large `l`.
- Dependency role: none in the proof of the `k = 4` rate. The paper is cited
  to prevent the historical `k = 3` question from being described as open and
  to keep the two subproblems typed separately.

The source was read at content level through its arXiv HTML rendering, including
the statement, Korsky injectivity input, OpenAI-construction input, base-three
construction, and proof of the limiting corollary.
