# Contributing

Contributions should identify the exact claim or dependency they affect and
include a proof, counterexample, formal certificate, or reproducible bounded
calculation. A computation must state its domain; a finite check does not prove
an unbounded statement.

Provenance corrections are especially welcome. The original Reddit
contributor may request any public credit form they prefer; the maintainers can
privately validate the original account from contemporaneous moderation
mod-mail or notification evidence. The proof is attributed to that contributor.

Do not add private identity evidence, copied chat transcripts, or copyrighted
bulk source text. Cite public locators and restate mathematics independently.
